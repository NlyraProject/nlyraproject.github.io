How it works

  1. LockYour NLYRA goes into the bridge adapter on Robinhood Chain. It never leaves the chain.
  2. VerifyLayerZero Labs and Nethermind each confirm the message. Both are required, every time.
  3. MintThe same amount appears in your wallet on Arbitrum One or BNB Chain. Coming back burns it and unlocks the original.

Only these contracts are official

Any other token called "NLYRA" on Arbitrum or BNB Chain is fake. Check the address before you buy, add a token to your wallet or approve anything.

    Daily limit

    Each direction has a cap of 20,000,000 NLYRA per 24 hours. The cap frees up gradually as time passes, so if a transfer is above what is available now, try a smaller amount or wait. A transfer over the limit is rejected before it is sent, so nothing is lost.

    What is dust?

    NLYRA has 18 decimals, but LayerZero bridges carry amounts with 6 decimals (the OFT "shared decimals"). Anything smaller than 0.000001 NLYRA cannot cross and simply stays in your wallet. There is no other slippage: you receive exactly the amount shown.

    Fees

    The bridge charges no NLYRA fee. You pay the LayerZero messaging fee in the gas token of the source chain (about 0.0001 ETH from Robinhood Chain or Arbitrum, about 0.0004 BNB from BNB Chain) plus normal network gas. On Robinhood Chain you first approve the bridge to move your NLYRA (one extra transaction). On Arbitrum no approval is needed.

    Security

    Both contracts are open source and verified. Two independent verifiers must confirm every message, and the message libraries are pinned so a change on LayerZero's side cannot silently affect NLYRA. Nobody at NLYRA can move your tokens: the adapter only releases NLYRA against a verified message. Bridging still carries smart-contract risk; bridge only what you can afford to lose.
